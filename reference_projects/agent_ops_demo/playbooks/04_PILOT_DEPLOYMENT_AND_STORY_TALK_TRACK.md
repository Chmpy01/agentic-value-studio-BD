# 90-day controlled pilot and partner demonstration

## Weeks 0–2: agree the value intent

Executive discovery, business outcome, accountable sponsor, authority boundary, site/process scope, top use-case hypothesis, source access and privacy posture. Sign a provisional **Value Intent Contract** and an evidence requirements record.

## Weeks 3–5: connect in read-only shadow mode

Use authorised adapters for aggregated source signals. Create entity mapping and event quality controls. Reconcile sampling, clock skew, source conflicts, sensitive data and baseline availability. Emit normalised events but **never actuate**.

## Weeks 6–8: introduce agent choreography

Enable bounded context resolution, hypothesis ranking, planning, and a separate policy engine. Produce tickets or proposed decisions in a test workspace. Review proposed actions with facilities/operations, CIO/security, HR/privacy, finance, specialist OT/FM partners.

## Weeks 9–10: human-approved closed-loop trial

Controlled, reversible non-safety intervention within the approved scope. Record approvals, work orders, relevant costs, counterfactual assumptions and service quality safeguards.

## Weeks 11–13: evidence and GTM decision

Independent measurement, verify customer outcomes and adverse effects, sponsor sign-off, cost-to-serve economics, expand/stop decision, offer and partner ownership model. Produce a graph update, not merely an AI activity report.

## 6-minute partner storyboard

**00:00 — Show why product dashboards fragment the customer story.** An occupancy heatmap is informative but not yet enterprise value.

**00:50 — Show the enterprise intent.** Board estate cost and carbon strategy cascades to HR, FM, IT, finance and occupants.

**01:40 — Hit Play in Agentic Value Operations.** The Signal Steward handles new data; the Context agent resolves entity and dependencies; the Value Reasoner identifies what may matter.

**02:40 — Freeze at Guard.** Demonstrate what the model is not allowed to do. Discuss safety, privacy and customer accountability.

**03:40 — Approve the simulated ticket.** Explain where the action broker would integrate into authorised business workflows, with independent receipt and exception handling.

**04:40 — Show Value Assurance.** Say clearly that observations are not savings. Follow the designed measurement and benefit sign-off path.

**05:30 — Partner monetisation.** From advisory and interoperability to recurring operations, assurance and multi-site expansion; demonstrate retail and manufacturing variations.

## Decision questions for partner leadership

- Which agent roles can we realistically operate and support at margin?
- Where do we integrate Cisco data and ecosystem applications without pretending they are a unified product?
- What does the customer already own that should remain its system of record?
- Who holds liability, response ownership and benefit sign-off for outcome-linked services?
