# 01 — Executive Discovery Conversation

**Goal:** Find an enterprise-level outcome worth investing in before showing a product demo. 45–60 minute facilitated opening, followed by deeper persona interviews.

## Opening conversation

1. **Strategy:** Which three board priorities must change over 12–36 months? What has been promised to investors, employees, customers or regulators?
2. **Economics:** Where does the money come from, go, or get exposed to risk? Which P&L/capex or cash-flow line changes if we succeed?
3. **Value streams:** Which end-to-end journeys create that result (e.g., customer conversion, production throughput, talent experience, portfolio optimisation)?
4. **Operating reality:** Which departments jointly influence the result? Where do their incentives, data definitions or planning cadences conflict?
5. **Signals:** Which recurring operational facts could confirm, challenge or explain the problem? Who owns each system and can authorise use?
6. **Decisions:** What practical decision would evidence change? What would you *not* do if the hypothesis fails?
7. **Measurement:** Who signs off the baseline, benefits calculation, approval threshold and post-change outcome?
8. **Constraints:** Which accessibility, health, labour, safety, cyber, regulatory or data limits must the intervention respect?

## Six-step interview format

| Phase | Time | Prompt | Capture |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Context | 8m | What must the enterprise achieve? | 3 board objectives |
| Economic exposure | 8m | Where is the value leakage or growth constraint? | P&L, asset, experience, risk pools |
| Cross-functional operating model | 10m | Who owns the decision, data and delivery? | Role map / RACI |
| Observed signals | 10m | What evidence already exists? | Systems, granularity, access, confidence |
| One testable hypothesis | 12m | What would a controlled pilot try to prove? | Hypothesis, metric, owner, control group |
| Next action | 7m | What is the smallest safe next step? | Approval gate, access requests, workstream owners |

## Persona overlay

- **Board / CEO:** growth, capital efficiency, continuity, stakeholder trust.
- **CFO:** P&L owners, payback, avoided cost versus cashable cost, cash cycle, accounting and benefit attribution.
- **Business operations / GM:** demand, workflow, capacity, quality and customer outcomes.
- **HR / experience:** adoption, access needs, workplace effectiveness; avoid unsafe individual productivity inference.
- **Real estate / facilities:** portfolio planning, space, energy, occupancy, technical readiness and change costs.
- **CIO / CISO / OT:** approved data boundary, architecture, observability, interoperability, safety and resilience.
- **Sustainability / governance:** evidence for reporting, energy / carbon baselines, standards and ownership.
- **Partner leaders:** service operating model, cost-to-serve, margin, accountability, recurring value, incentives.
