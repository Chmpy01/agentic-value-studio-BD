## Calculation (for illustrative packs)

- `Gross annual opportunity = annual addressable pool × improvement % × capture factor`.
- `Net annual customer value = gross annual opportunity − annual customer recurring charges`.
- `Year-one customer value = net annual value − upfront implementation and change costs`.
- `Simple payback (months) = upfront cost / (net annual value ÷ 12)` when net annual value is positive.

This is **not NPV, ROI assurance or revenue recognition accounting**. For a funded decision add discounting, taxes if material, cash timing, one-offs, implementation delays, cannibalisation, inflation, volume mix, costs of change, cost-to-serve and credible downside scenarios.

## Multi-value-stream caution

Do not add energy savings, occupancy savings, workforce productivity and asset efficiency without identifying overlap, causality and which costs disappear from the P&L. Reduced floor occupancy alone does not create cash savings unless a lease, operating scope or usage-based cost can change.

## Hypothesis test design prompt

```text
For each draft hypothesis, propose a defensible validation design and a
baseline-to-outcome measurement plan. Identify confounders, an appropriate
control/comparison, baseline duration, owner and confidence limitation.
Separate customer cash benefits, non-cash productivity/experience metrics,
risk exposure and partner revenue. Produce three scenarios (downside/base/
upside) with transparent assumptions, and do not double count linked effects.
Flag any claim that cannot be validated from permitted evidence.
```

**Stage gate:** Customer benefit owners and finance approve the business case before capability and commercial packaging.
